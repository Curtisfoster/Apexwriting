Frequently asked questions

How does ordering work?

Send your requirements via the form, email, or WhatsApp. We reply with a price and timeline. After you confirm, we assign a writer and deliver by the agreed deadline.

What is the starting price?

From $10 for 300 words. Final price depends on academic level, deadline, subject complexity, and length. You always receive a clear quote before you commit.

Are papers human-written?

Yes. We do not use AI to generate papers. Work is written by people. You can request an originality report with your order.

How do free revisions work?

Within 14 days of delivery, you can request changes related to the original brief at no extra cost. Scope changes may need a new quote.

What if you miss the deadline?

Contact us immediately. Depending on the situation you may receive a partial or full refund under our Refund Policy, or priority completion.

Do you have a refund policy?

Yes. See our full Refund Policy.
