Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Apex Academic Writing (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, WhatsApp number)
  • Order details (topic, requirements, academic level, deadlines)
  • Messages you send us via email or WhatsApp
  • Basic technical data (IP address, browser type) if you visit our website

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests and complete orders
  • To communicate about deadlines, revisions, and delivery
  • To improve our service
  • To comply with legal obligations where required

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share order details only with the writer assigned to your project, under confidentiality expectations. We may use service providers (e.g. email, hosting) who process data on our behalf.

4. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Communication via WhatsApp and email is subject to the security of those platforms.

5. Retention

We keep order-related information as long as needed to provide the service and handle follow-ups, then delete or anonymize it when no longer required.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data by emailing hello@apexacademicwriting.com.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@apexacademicwriting.com
